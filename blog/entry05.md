## **Content**
The tool I learned throughout the stretch of times is Aframes ausing fAframes school, I better understood how to use it & all of it's perks to help me with my project. Aframes is a software that allows you to create 3D models using geometric shapes, and Aframes combines a mix of VR with interactivity with some of the properties you can use.

Blog 4 was mostly about the basics, and in this blog I will document how I would use Aframes for my freedom project. On my wireframe, Mr Mueller asked us to incorporate our tool (my tool is Aframes), into our website & I decided to make a 3D model of my app / invention, Mediscan+. It's still not fully completed, nor have I started on it but I'd rather chip away on the basics and fundamentals than focusing on a project so big so, this is what I've learned.

I learned about positioning & core priminatives (which is basically shapes), along with Camera movement. I learned this using Aframes schools, and youtube videos, which in my opinion is the best way to do this because everythings layed out in a way that it's comprehensible & easy to learn.

Here's a piece of code I made / a way I tinkered with Aframe.
```
<a-scene>
  <a-box position="0 1 -4" color="tomato"></a-box>
 
  <a-entity position="0 1.6 0">
    <a-camera wasd-controls look-controls></a-camera>
  </a-entity>

  <a-sky color="#a3d0ff"></a-sky>
</a-scene>
```
